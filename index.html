How to Play

  • Objective: Match as many neon pairs as possible in 60 seconds.
  • Growing grid: Round 1 is a 4×4 square. Every round you clear adds +2 pairs — the grid grows into bigger rectangles (5×4, 6×4, 7×4, 8×4…).
  • Controls: Tap / click cards. Keyboard: Tab + Enter or Space to flip, arrow keys to move. Mute anytime with 🔊.
  • Scoring: Pair matched = +20. Consecutive matches add +5 per streak level (up to +25 extra).
  • Penalty: Mismatch = −5 (score never drops below 0) and resets your streak.
  • Round clear: Match every pair for +50 and the next, bigger board!
  • The timer starts only when you press Start Game and stops automatically at 0.
